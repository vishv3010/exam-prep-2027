# The first month, day by day

**Purpose of this file: remove every decision from 5:30am.** You should never wake up and
wonder what to open. Nobody fails the morning block because they lack a syllabus — they fail
because the first ten minutes require a choice, and at 5:30am a choice is a chance to go back
to bed.

---

## The ramp — read this before anything else

**Do not start at 1h45.** Starting at full volume on day one is the single most common way this
fails: it works for four days, feels heroic, and collapses in the second week.

| Week | Deep block | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **60 min** | Prove the wake-up. Content is almost irrelevant |
| 2 | **80 min** | Extend it |
| 3 | **105 min** (full) | Full volume |
| 4 | **105 min** | Hold it |

**Week 1's only real target is getting out of bed five times.** If you do 60 easy minutes for
five days, week 1 is a complete success even if you learn almost nothing. If you do three
brilliant 2-hour sessions and then miss Thursday and Friday, it is a failure. The habit is the
asset. Content is what the habit later carries.

---

## Why Polity comes first in the night block

Of every topic across all three exams, **Polity has the highest shared value**:

- CDS General Knowledge — a large, reliable chunk
- **Gujarat PSI — an entire Constitution and legal-matters section, with a 40% independent cut-off**
- SSC CGL General Awareness — recurring

Nothing else you could study pays into all three this heavily. So it goes first, while
motivation is highest and before anything has a chance to feel like a slog.

## Week 0 — Thu 27 to Sun 30 August · Setup

No studying. Clear the runway so Monday has no excuses.

- [ ] **Thu 27** — **Audit the books you already own** (`books.md`) — you need far less than the
      original list said. Order only Word Power Made Easy and a Gujarat GK book. Download 10
      years of CDS past papers from upsc.gov.in into `reference/`, and print one full set for
      13 September.
- [ ] **Fri 28** — Book the medical (`physical.md`). Set the 5:15 alarm — put the phone across
      the room, not beside the bed.
- [ ] **Sat 29** — First easy run, 20 minutes, conversational pace. Buy an OMR practice pad.
- [ ] **Sun 30** — Read one Gujarati editorial, start `log/gujarati-vocab.md`. Set out
      tomorrow's books on the table tonight, open to the right page.

> Laying the book out open, the night before, is not a gimmick. It removes the last decision
> between waking and starting.

### 🎯 Sunday 13 September — the cold diagnostic

The same day CDS II 2026 runs. Sit **a full CDS past paper, cold, at real timing.**

- 09:00–11:00 English · 12:00–14:00 GK · 15:00–17:00 Elementary Maths
- No notes. No phone. No pausing. Mark it honestly with the official key.
- Record all three scores in `log/mocks.md`.

**It will feel bad. That is the entire point.** This number is not a judgement, it is a
baseline — and every score for the next seven months is measured against it. Skipping this
because you "aren't ready yet" is exactly backwards: an unprepared diagnostic is the only
honest one you will ever get.

### Sunday 27 September — the month-end check

Two numbers, both honest:

1. **Mock #2 against the 13 Sep diagnostic.** Any movement at all after four weeks is
   correct — this early it should be small. A large jump usually means the second paper was
   easier, not that you improved.
2. **Your first 5 km time.** Whatever it is, it is the baseline. Log it.

**The real question is neither of those.** It is: *did the 5:30 block run 20 of 25 weekdays?*

If yes, Phase 0 has succeeded regardless of the scores, and Phase 1 can safely double the
content. If no, **do not add material in October.** Repeat the ramp instead and fix the
wake-up, because every later phase is built on top of that block existing.

## Rules for the month

1. **Miss a day, never miss two.** One is noise; two is how a habit dies.
2. **The night block never gets new hard concepts.** Tired brains revise; they do not learn.
3. **Log every day in `log/daily.md`, even the bad ones** — especially the bad ones. A log with
   gaps in it stops being evidence and starts being decoration.
4. **Do not buy more books this month.** You have not finished the ones you have.
5. **Protect sleep over the night block.** Always. Every time.
