# Phases — 26 Aug 2026 → 11 Apr 2027

---

## Phase 0 — September 2026 · Baseline (weeks 1–5)

**The point of this phase is measurement, not progress.** You cannot plan around a skill level
you have not measured. Do not skip to studying.

- ⚠️ **Day 1: confirm the SSC CGL 2026 application** (`ssc-cgl-2026-sprint.md`). If it went
  through, Tier 1 is in September and this month belongs to it — CDS Phase 0 runs in parallel
  at reduced volume, and resumes properly the day after the CGL exam
- **Week 1: sit a full CDS past paper cold.** All three papers, real timing, no preparation.
  It will feel bad. That is the point — it is your map, and every later score is measured
  against it. Do this on **13 Sep**, the day CDS II 2026 runs.
- Start Maths from arithmetic. Percentages, ratio, time-speed-distance
- Start English grammar spine + 15 vocabulary words/day
- Start the GK habit: Lucent + 30 min newspaper, capped
- **Start running** (`physical.md`) and **start the Gujarati drip** (`gujarati.md`)
- **Book the medical check**
- Download 10 years of CDS past papers from `upsc.gov.in` into `reference/`

**Exit test:** you know your real starting score in all three papers, and the 5:30am block
has run for 20 of 25 weekdays. If the habit didn't hold, fix the habit before adding material.

## Phase 1 — October–November 2026 · Core build (weeks 6–13)

The heavy-lifting phase. Full syllabus, first pass.

- **Maths:** complete the entire syllabus once — Arithmetic → Algebra → Geometry → Mensuration
  → Trigonometry → Statistics. As a B.E. graduate you should move fast here. Do not let
  familiarity make you skip practice; the exam tests speed, not comprehension
- **English:** grammar complete, comprehension speed drills, vocabulary continues daily
- **GK:** History → Polity → Geography → Economy → Science, plus rolling current affairs
- First **sectional tests** from week 8. One subject at a time, full length, timed
- Running moves into the Build block

📌 **2 December: CDS I 2027 notification.** Read it the day it drops. Verify the age window,
the pattern, and the vacancy split against everything in these files.

**Exit test:** every Maths and GK topic in `syllabus-tracker.md` ticked once. Sectional
scores rising. ~200 hours logged.

## Phase 2 — December 2026–January 2027 · Consolidate + apply (weeks 14–21)

- 📌 **Apply for CDS I 2027 in the first week of December.** Deadline is 22 Dec.
  Apply for **all four entries** — IMA, INA, AFA, OTA. One form, four chances.
  Do not wait for the deadline; the portal collapses in the last 48 hours, every single year
- Second pass over the full syllabus, weighted to whatever the mocks say is weak
- **Weekly full mocks begin.** Sunday morning, real timing, no pauses
- 🎯 **SSB preparation starts here** — 2 hrs/week minimum. OIR, PPDT, TAT, WAT, SRT
- Gujarati: add one written paragraph per week

**Exit test:** application submitted and confirmed. Mock scores clearing the historical
cutoff band with margin. SSB structure understood, not just heard of.

---

## Phase 3 — February–March 2027 · Test-heavy (weeks 22–30)

The phase where scores actually move. **Revision and testing, not new material.**

- **2 full mocks per week** + full error-log review after each
- Revision cycles over weak topics, driven entirely by the error log
- **SSB ramps to 4 hrs/week** — this is where you build real OLQ evidence from your own life:
  the job, the degree, the app you shipped, the gym. Interviewers ask for specifics, and
  "I am a leader" is not an answer. A story is
- Running peaks — 5 km time trial monthly
- Defence current affairs gets its own weekly slot; it is high-yield and CDS-specific

**Exit test:** consistent mock scores well above the cutoff. Error log shrinking, not growing.

---

## Phase 4 — April 2027 · Taper (weeks 31–32)

- **No new material after 25 March.** None. New material this late adds anxiety, not marks
- Past papers and revision notes only
- Reduce training volume, keep frequency. Do not injure yourself in the last fortnight
- Sleep goes back to 7+ hours for the final ten days — this is worth more marks than the
  studying it replaces
- Logistics: admit card, ID, centre location, travel, the route, the alarm

📌 **11 April 2027 — CDS I 2027 written.**
