# The daily template

Built around: job 8:30am–8:10pm (incl. travel), gym 1hr+, gym timing flexible.

## Weekday — 3h05m study + 1h training

| Time | Block | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 5:15 | wake | — |
| **5:30–7:15** | **DEEP (1h45)** | Maths (Mon/Wed/Fri) or English (Tue/Thu). Hardest material, freshest brain. No phone. **Ramp in over 4 weeks — see `week-01-04.md`. Do not start at 1h45.** |
| 7:15–8:15 | breakfast, ready | — |
| 8:30–~9:30 | **commute** | Current-affairs audio, GK flashcards. Time you are already spending. |
| 9:30–7:00 | work | — |
| ~7:00–8:10 | **commute** | Gujarati editorial on phone, or audio revision |
| 8:30–9:30 | **gym / run** | See `physical.md` — running must enter the rotation |
| 9:30–10:15 | dinner, decompress | — |
| **10:15–11:20** | **LIGHT (1h05)** | Mon/Wed/Fri → CDS GK + current affairs. **Tue/Thu → PSI** (Gujarat GK, Constitution & legal, psychology/sociology). **Never new hard concepts.** |
| 11:45 | sleep | — |

**Sleep is 6h15. That is the tightest part of this plan.**
If you cannot hold it, **cut the night block to 45 minutes — never the sleep.**
Sleep debt destroys retention silently: you keep studying, you stop remembering, and you will not
notice for six weeks. Protecting sleep is not slacking, it is the highest-leverage thing here.

## Weekly allocation

With PSI as a co-anchor, the ~25 hours split like this:

| Stream | Hrs/wk | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **CDS core** — Maths, English | ~9 | The protected 5:30am deep block, every weekday |
| **Shared GK + current affairs** | ~5 | Mon/Wed/Fri nights + both commutes. Serves all three exams |
| **PSI-only** — Gujarat GK, legal, psych/socio | ~4 | Tue/Thu nights + a Saturday block |
| **Gujarati** | ~1.5 | 10 min daily + weekly writing from December |
| **Testing & review** | ~5 | Weekends |

**The deep block stays CDS.** That is the one thing PSI does not get to take, because the
CDS window is the one that closes. Everything else flexes.

## Saturday — 5–6 hrs
- Morning: 3 hrs sectional tests (one subject, full length, timed)
- Afternoon: 1 hr review — every wrong answer goes in the error log
- Afternoon: 1 hr **PSI block** — Gujarat GK, or legal matters
- Long run

## Sunday — 4–5 hrs
- Morning: **full mock under real exam timing.** Same start time as the real exam. No pauses.
  Alternate weeks: CDS pattern / PSI Paper-1 pattern **on an OMR sheet, drilling Option E**
- Afternoon: error-log review + the week's weakest topic
- From December: one formal Gujarati paragraph written
- Plan the coming week

## The numbers

~15.5 hrs weekdays + ~10 hrs weekend = **~25 hrs/week**
× 32 weeks to 11 April 2027 = **~800 focused hours**

That is comfortably enough for CDS. This plan does not require heroics — it requires that
you actually do the 5:30am block. That block is the entire plan. Everything else is support.

## The one rule

**Miss a day, never miss two.** A missed day is noise. Two missed days is how a habit dies.
