# Physical

You already train 1hr+/day. That is a real head start — but it solves the wrong half
of the problem.

## The gap

**Gym strength ≠ running endurance.** They are different energy systems. Lifters routinely
fail 5km time trials that untrained runners pass. Every one of your three targets tests
endurance, not the bench press:

| Target | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Gujarat PSI PET (male)** | **5000 m in 25 minutes** — qualifying, pass/fail |
| **CDS / SSB** | No formal PET, but 5 days of GTO ground tasks, obstacles, running |
| **CBI SI (via CGL)** | Physical standards + endurance test |

5000 m in 25 min is a **5:00/km pace**. For someone who lifts but does not run, that is a
genuine test on day one and completely routine after three months of structured running.

## The conversion — start this week

Keep lifting. Add running. Do not replace one with the other.

| Phase | Weeks | Running |
|---|---|---|
| Base | 1–4 | 3×/week, 20–30 min easy. Conversational pace. Do not race it |
| Build | 5–12 | 3×/week — one long (6–8 km), one interval (6×400 m), one easy |
| Sharpen | 13–24 | Add a monthly **5 km time trial**. Log every time in `log/` |
| Maintain | ongoing | 2–3×/week. Never let the base disappear |

Target: comfortably under 23 min by December. That gives four minutes of margin, and margin
is what survives a bad day, a bad track, and exam-morning nerves.

## Medical — do this in September, not later

Every one of these has a medical stage, and **medical rejection after clearing a written exam
is the most painful way to lose a year.** Some disqualifiers are fixable with a year's notice.
Almost none are fixable with a week's notice.

Get a full check now:

- [ ] **Vision** — the strictest gate. Know your exact numbers for each eye, and know
      the current rules on spectacles and on refractive surgery for each entry
- [ ] **Height / weight / chest** — chest expansion is measured; know your numbers
- [ ] **Hearing**
- [ ] **BP, resting heart rate**
- [ ] **Teeth** — genuinely a rejection cause at SSB medical
- [ ] **Flat feet, knock knees, bow legs** — common silent rejections
- [ ] **Old fractures, surgeries, scars** — get documentation now
- [ ] **Blood work** — routine panel

⚠️ Verify current standards against the **official notification** for each exam. Medical
standards differ between IMA, INA, AFA and the police services, and they are revised.
Do not rely on a coaching website — read the notification annexure.

## Why September

If something turns up, you have seven months to treat it or to re-weight the plan toward the
targets it does not block. Finding out in April 2027 costs you the entire cycle.
