# Gujarat GK & the PSI-only content

This is the material that **only** PSI needs — everything else in your week is shared with CDS
and CGL. It is roughly 4 hours a week (Tue/Thu nights + a Saturday block), and it is the
difference between a notification finding you ready and finding you starting.

**Cover it once by December. Then revise monthly and never let it go cold.**

> Use this file as the map, and a Gujarat GK book (Liberty or World Inbox) as the territory.
> Verify names, dates and numbers against the book — this is a sequencing guide, not a source.

---

## Sequence — why this order

**Geography first, then history, then culture, then economy.** Geography is the scaffolding:
once districts, rivers and regions are in your head, historical events and industries have
somewhere to attach. Learning Gujarat history before Gujarat geography means memorising
place-names that mean nothing to you yet.

---

## 1 · Geography — the scaffolding

- **Formation** — 1 May 1960, from the bifurcation of Bombay State. Capital Gandhinagar
  (Ahmedabad initially). First Chief Minister Jivraj Mehta
- **Districts** — 33. Learn them by region, never as a flat list
- **The five regions** — Kutch · Saurashtra · North Gujarat · Central Gujarat · South Gujarat
- **Coastline** — approximately 1,600 km, **the longest of any Indian state.** Understand why
  that matters for policing: ports, marine borders, smuggling, coastal security
- **Rivers** — Narmada, Tapi, Sabarmati, Mahi, Banas, Saraswati. Know which region each serves
- **Gulfs and the Rann** — Gulf of Khambhat, Gulf of Kutch, Great and Little Rann
- **Protected areas** — Gir (Asiatic lion) · Wild Ass Sanctuary, Little Rann · Velavadar
  (blackbuck) · Nal Sarovar · Marine National Park, Gulf of Kutch
- **Neighbours** — Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, and the **international border with
  Pakistan** in Kutch. High-yield for a police paper

## 2 · History

- **Indus Valley in Gujarat** — Lothal (dockyard), Dholavira, Rangpur, Surkotada
- **Early dynasties** — Maitrakas of Vallabhi · Solanki / Chaulukya at Anhilwad Patan
  (Siddhraj Jaysinh, Kumarpal) · Vaghelas
- **Gujarat Sultanate** — Ahmed Shah founds Ahmedabad, 1411
- **Mughal, Maratha, British** periods and the princely states
- **Freedom struggle — the highest-yield section of all:**
  - Gandhi — Sabarmati Ashram, **Dandi March 1930**
  - **Sardar Patel** — Kheda 1918, **Bardoli Satyagraha 1928** (where he earned "Sardar"),
    and the integration of the princely states
  - Indulal Yagnik and the **Mahagujarat Movement** that produced the state itself

## 3 · Culture

- **Festivals** — Navratri and Garba · Uttarayan (Makar Sankranti) · Rann Utsav · Tarnetar
- **Crafts** — Patola of Patan · Bandhani · Kutch embroidery · Rogan art
- **Folk forms** — Bhavai theatre · Garba · Dandiya Raas
- **Literature** — Narsinh Mehta · Premanand · Narmad · Govardhanram Tripathi
  (*Saraswatichandra*) · Jhaverchand Meghani · Umashankar Joshi · Pannalal Patel · Rajendra Shah
- **Sites** — Rani ki Vav (Patan) · Modhera Sun Temple · Champaner · Somnath · Dwarka ·
  Statue of Unity. Know which are UNESCO World Heritage

## 4 · Economy

- Petrochemicals and refining — Jamnagar · Chemicals and pharma
- **Diamonds and textiles — Surat** · Textiles — Ahmedabad
- **Dairy — Amul and the Anand cooperative model.** A recurring favourite
- **Ports — Kandla/Deendayal, Mundra.** Ties back to the coastline
- Salt production · GIFT City · major state schemes and current initiatives

## 5 · Institutions & administration

- Gujarat Vidhan Sabha — **182 seats** · Lok Sabha — **26** · Rajya Sabha — **11**
- Gujarat High Court · GPSC · GPRB
- District administration: Collector, DDO, and **the police hierarchy — DGP, IG, DIG, SP, DySP,
  PI, PSI.** Know exactly where the post you are applying for sits, and what it commands.
  This is interview and paper material both

## The other PSI-only sections

### Constitution & legal matters

Overlaps your Polity study, so it is cheaper than it looks — the extra is the police-facing part:

- Fundamental Rights and Duties, DPSP *(shared with CDS/CGL)*
- **IPC / BNS basics** — offences against body, property, public order
- **CrPC / BNSS basics** — arrest, FIR, investigation, custody, bail, search and seizure
- **Evidence Act basics** · Police powers and their limits
- Gujarat Police Act, and human-rights obligations in policing

> Note the transition from IPC/CrPC/Evidence Act to the new **BNS / BNSS / BSA** codes.
> Check which the notification examines before investing heavily in either.

### Psychology & Sociology

**Unusual on a police paper, genuinely tested, and widely skipped — which is exactly why it is
worth points.** Everyone crams Gujarat GK; far fewer touch this.

- **Psychology** — perception, learning, memory, motivation, emotion, personality, stress,
  attitude and prejudice, group behaviour, crowd psychology
- **Sociology** — family, marriage, kinship · caste and class · social stratification ·
  social change and mobility · urbanisation · social problems: crime, delinquency, addiction,
  domestic violence

Connect it back to policing as you learn it — crowd control, interrogation, community
relations, juvenile handling. It makes the material stick, and it is precisely the framing an
interview board rewards.
