Twenty-five weekdays from 31 August to 2 October. Tap a day when the morning block actually happened. Twenty out of twenty-five and Phase 1 can safely double the content — fewer, and you repeat the ramp instead of adding material.

The first month · 31 Aug – 27 Sep

Every day already decided

Nobody fails the morning block for lack of a syllabus. They fail because the first ten minutes require a choice, and at 5:30am a choice is a chance to go back to bed. Today's row is highlighted.

Read this before day one

Do not start at 1h45. Ramp it.

Full volume on day one works for four days, feels heroic, and collapses in week two. Week 1 is 60 minutes, week 2 is 80, weeks 3–4 are the full 105.

Week 1's only real target is getting out of bed five times. Five easy 60-minute sessions is a complete success even if you learn almost nothing. Three brilliant two-hour sessions followed by a missed Thursday and Friday is a failure. The habit is the asset; content is what it later carries.

Why Polity leads the night block, and geography leads the PSI block

Polity has the highest shared value of any topic you could start with — a large chunk of CDS General Knowledge, recurring in CGL General Awareness, and an entire section of PSI Paper 1 with its own 40% cut-off. Nothing else pays into all three this heavily, so it goes first, while motivation is highest.

Gujarat geography is scaffolding. Once districts, rivers and regions are in your head, historical events and industries have somewhere to attach. Learn Gujarat history first and you are memorising place-names that mean nothing yet.

CDS holds the morning block

Not because it matters more. Because roughly four IMA/INA attempts remain before age closes it in 2029, while PSI stays open to 35. The perishable one gets the protected hours.

Your degree buys three extra doors

One application covers IMA, INA, AFA and OTA. INA requires an engineering degree; AFA requires engineering or 10+2 with PCM. Non-engineering graduates compete for two entries. You compete for four.

PSI is a readiness problem

858 posts in one Gujarat cycle against 451 CDS vacancies nationally, and no SSB. But no date either — so it is about standing ready, not waiting. A notification is a 60–90 day sprint.

The SSB is the real filter

CDS written cutoffs run ~20–25%. The five-day interview converts in single digits, and most candidates walk in cold. SSB prep starts December, not on a call letter.

⚠ The PSI trap that CDS and SSC habits will walk you into

Gujarat deducts 0.25 for a wrong answer and 0.25 for any question left completely blank. Every question carries a fifth option, E — “Not Attempted”, which costs nothing. Marking nothing at all costs you.

In CDS and SSC, skipping is free. Anyone drilled on those walks in with the habit of leaving blanks and bleeds marks on questions they never even read. Answer it, or mark E. Never leave a row empty — drill it on real OMR sheets until it is automatic.

Also: 40% minimum in each part of Paper 1, independently. A brilliant GK score does not rescue a failed Constitution section, so no part can be punted.

⚠ Where an old book misleads

BNS, BNSS and BSA replaced the IPC, CrPC and Evidence Act from 1 July 2024, with different section numbers. Affects nothing on your shelf — only PSI legal material you buy.

Audit your CDS book

Ten minutes. Pattern page shows 120/120/100? Newest past paper year — download everything after it. Grammar section? That decides whether Wren & Martin is worth buying at all.

Verify before relying on it

Every date and standard here was checked against live sources on 26 August 2026, but exam patterns, age relaxations and physical standards are revised year to year. The age window is built from the published pattern, not from the 2027 notification itself. Re-verify against the actual notification at upsc.gov.in, ssc.gov.in and gprb.gujarat.gov.in when each drops — and read the notification annexure rather than a coaching website.
