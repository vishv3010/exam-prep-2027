## Why the age of these books mostly doesn't matter

**Lucent GK.** Static general knowledge — ancient and medieval history, geography, polity
structure, basic science — does not change year to year. That is 80%+ of the book's value and
all of it is still good. What *has* aged: current affairs sections, latest appointments, sports
records, census and economic figures. Those were never the reason to own Lucent. **Read it for
the static core and get current affairs from a newspaper.**

**Your CDS book.** The exam pattern has been stable for years — English 120 questions/100 marks,
GK 120/100, Elementary Maths 100/100, negative marking 1/3. A book 1–2 years old is describing
the same exam you will sit. The only genuinely stale part is its past-paper section, and
**UPSC publishes every paper with the official answer key for free at upsc.gov.in.** Download
the last three years and you have completely closed that gap at zero cost.

**Your NCERTs — the best surprise here.** CDS Elementary Mathematics is class 9–10 level, which
means NCERT Class 9 and 10 Maths is not a fallback, it is a *primary source* — and better for
building concepts than the practice-volume books, which assume you already understand and just
want drills. NCERT Science and Social Science at that level is also a clean, uncluttered
foundation for the GK paper.

**The right way to use them together:**
> **NCERT to understand it → your CDS book to drill it at exam pattern → past papers to test it.**
> Three roles, three books, all already in your possession.

## ⚠️ The one place where an old book will actively mislead you

**Criminal law, for the PSI stream.** The **BNS, BNSS and BSA replaced the IPC, CrPC and
Evidence Act with effect from 1 July 2024.** A book printed before that — or a hastily revised
one — may still teach the old codes, with different section numbers throughout.

This does not affect Lucent, your CDS book or the NCERTs. It affects **only** the legal-matters
material you buy for PSI. When you buy it:

- [ ] Confirm it teaches **BNS / BNSS / BSA**, not only IPC / CrPC
- [ ] When the GPRB notification drops, **check which the syllabus names** and follow that

Section numbers are exactly the kind of thing an exam tests and an outdated book gets wrong.
This is the one purchase where edition genuinely matters.
