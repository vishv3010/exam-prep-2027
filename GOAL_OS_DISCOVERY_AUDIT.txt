C. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & REAL BEHAVIOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Application Flow:
   User opens site/index.html. The dashboard checks Date(), displays static task
   strings from SCHED, and renders the 25-day habit tracker from localStorage['cds2027.v1'].
   Clicking the 'PSI Engine' tab hides the dashboard container (display: none)
   and mounts the PSI UI (#psi-root).

2. Data & Storage Flow:
   The two subsystems maintain independent lifecycles. If you score 100% in a
   PSI Constitution drill, the CDS Dashboard does not register that Polity was studied.

3. Question-Selection Flow:
   QuestionEngine.prototype.buildMetro40Session requests 40 questions (25% due,
   20% mistakes, 25% weak, 30% unseen). Because the entire database has only 45
   questions, the selector immediately exhausts unseen items and falls back to
   dumping the entire database in random order.

4. Scoring & SRS Flow:
   Answers are evaluated instantly against question.answer (0-4 index). Incorrect
   attempts write a record to mistakes[q.id] and reset the Leitner card to Box 1
   (due = now + 1 day). Correct attempts advance the card to Box 2 (due = now + 3 days).

A. UPSC CDS I 2027 — INDIAN MILITARY ACADEMY (IMA)
Source: Official UPSC CDS Examination Notifications & Regulations
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Notification Status: Expected December 2026 (UPSC Annual Calendar). Exam Date: 11 April 2027.
- Eligibility (IMA): Unmarried male; Age 19 to 24 years at course commencement (January 2028).
  B.Tech degree is fully qualifying.
- Selection Scheme: Written Examination (300 Marks) + SSB Interview (300 Marks).

- Written Examination Structure (All 3 papers conducted on a single Sunday):
    1. Paper 1: English (120 Questions · 100 Marks · 2 Hours). Objective MCQ.
    2. Paper 2: General Knowledge (120 Questions · 100 Marks · 2 Hours). Objective MCQ.
       Science ~30%, Polity ~15-20%, History ~15-20%, Geography ~15-20%, Current Affairs ~15%.
    3. Paper 3: Elementary Mathematics (100 Questions · 100 Marks · 2 Hours). Objective MCQ.
       Arithmetic, Algebra, Trigonometry, Geometry, Mensuration, Statistics. Class 9-10
       standard, but demanding high calculation speed.

- Negative Marking: 1/3rd (0.333 marks) deducted per incorrect answer. Blank questions
  receive 0 penalty. No Option E.
- Qualifying Cutoffs: Minimum 20% qualifying marks in each paper independently
  (20/100 each in English, GK, Maths). Aggregate written cutoff for IMA is
  typically 130-145 / 300 marks.
- SSB Interview (300 Marks):
    * Stage 1 (Screening): Officer Intelligence Rating (OIR) test + Picture Perception
      & Discussion Test (PPDT). ~60-70% screened out on Day 1.
    * Stage 2 (4 Days): Psychology Battery (TAT, WAT, SRT, Self-Description),
      GTO Obstacles/Ground Tasks, Personal Interview, Board Conference.

B. GUJARAT POLICE ARMED PSI (GPRB)
Source: Official Gujarat Police Cadre (Class III) Recruitment Rules (Notification GPRB/202324/1)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Recruitment Status: Next notification expected late 2026/2027. Governed by the
  2024 Revised Recruitment Rules.
- Cadre Distinction: Armed PSI (હથિયારી પીએસઆઈ - SRPF / Armed Reserve) and
  Unarmed PSI (બિન-હથિયારી) take the EXACT SAME examination. Preferences are
  submitted during application.

- Stage 1: Physical (PET/PST):
  Qualifying Only (Pass/Fail) under 2024 rules. Male PET: 5,000 meters in 25:00 minutes.
  No graded marks awarded for faster times; clearing within 25 min qualifies candidate
  for written examinations.

- Stage 2: Main Written Examination (Total: 300 Marks across Two Papers):
    1. Paper 1: General Studies (MCQ) — 200 Marks · 3 Hours. Divided into two mandatory parts:
       * Part A (100 Marks): Reasoning & Data Interpretation (50 Marks) + Quantitative
         Aptitude (50 Marks). Must score MINIMUM 40% (40/100) independently.
       * Part B (100 Marks): Constitution of India (25 Marks) + Public Administration,
         History, Geography, Cultural Heritage, Current Affairs (75 Marks). Must score
         MINIMUM 40% (40/100) independently.
    2. Paper 2: Language Skills (Descriptive) — 100 Marks · 3 Hours · Handwritten Descriptive:
       * Gujarati Skills (70 Marks): Essay Writing, Precis Writing, Comprehension,
         Formal Report/Letter. Must score MINIMUM 40% (28/70).
       * English Skills (30 Marks): English-to-Gujarati Translation, Precis Writing,
         Comprehension. Must score MINIMUM 40% (12/30).

- Negative Marking & Option E:
  0.25 marks deducted for incorrect answers. 0.25 marks deducted for leaving a
  question blank. Option E ('(E) પ્રયાસ કરેલ નથી') carries 0 penalty.

- Law Status (Critical Finding):
  In the 2024 GPRB revised scheme, detailed standalone Law papers (IPC, CrPC, IEA)
  were REMOVED from the written exam and moved to Police Academy post-selection
  training. Core constitutional law, police powers, and basic legal principles
  remain tested under Paper 1 Part B.

5. CURRENT SYSTEM WEAKNESSES
================================================================================

1. The 'CDS Engine' Does Not Exist:
   The platform is advertised as a preparation engine for CDS and PSI. In reality,
   the engine only tests Gujarat PSI. CDS preparation is purely a static text calendar
   in HTML. There is no testing module for CDS Maths, English, or Science.

2. Severe Question Scarcity (45 Questions Total):
   An adaptive spaced repetition system requires at least 800-1,500 questions per exam
   stream to function. Attempting a 40-question 'Metro Session' on day two exposes the
   user to the same questions they solved on day one.

3. Data Siloing (cds2027.v1 vs psi_trainer.v1):
   The habit dashboard and the quiz engine operate in separate storage keys. Completing
   a quiz does not mark habit progress, and habit progress does not inform quiz selection.

4. Algorithm Misrepresentation:
   The code uses a rigid 5-box Leitner system, not SuperMemo SM-2. Cards reset to
   Box 1 immediately on failure, creating frustration loops rather than calculating
   variable memory decay (EF).

5. Rigid Calendar Scheduling:
   The schedule uses hardcoded date keys (SCHED['2026-09-23']). If you miss Monday,
   Tuesday does not adapt—it moves ahead to Tuesday's topic, leaving unaddressed
   knowledge gaps and breaking continuity.

6. Zero Math / Quantitative Module:
   Despite Quantitative Aptitude being 100 marks in CDS (33% of written) and 50 marks
   in Gujarat PSI Paper 1 Part A (mandatory 40% cutoff), the app does not contain
   a single math problem or formula trainer.

7. No Support for PSI Paper 2 (100 Marks Descriptive):
   Paper 2 determines the final merit list and carries a strict 40% cutoff. The app
   provides zero guidance, translation exercises, or precis structural templates.

8. Statistically Meaningless Diagnostic:
   The built-in diagnostic test pulls 10 questions across 4 subjects. A 10-question
   test has a +/-35% margin of error and provides zero actionable data on a candidate's
   actual baseline.

9. Data Corruption Bug:
   Line 255 and line 465 in site/data/psi/gujarat-gk.js share the identical ID 'ref_gk_adm_02'.

10. Metro Mode is Not Real-World Metro Optimized:
    The UI requires vertical scrolling, reading long feedback paragraphs, and tapping
    small buttons. True standing metro mode requires high-contrast, thumb-zone tap
    targets, zero vertical scrolling during questions, and audio options.

14. BIGGEST SYSTEMIC RISKS
================================================================================

1. The Software Feature Trap: Spending 40 hours perfecting CSS layouts, animations,
   or database migrations while solving zero math equations on paper. The software
   exists only to enforce study velocity; it cannot substitute for cognitive effort.
2. Unrealistic Energy Expectations: Assuming an engineer working an 11.5-hour day
   can sustain 3.5 hours of high-intensity study every night without cognitive burnout.
   The system must degrade gracefully: if tired, fall back to low-friction flashcards
   rather than skipping entirely.
3. SSB Interview Blindness: Focusing 100% on written cutoffs while ignoring physical
   fitness, English conversational fluency, and situational judgment. Day 1 SSB
   screening eliminates 65% of candidates.
4. Unauthentic Question Ingestion: Ingesting generic AI-generated MCQs that lack
   the distinct distractor patterns and testing style of UPSC and GPRB.

15. QUESTIONS FOR VISHV
================================================================================

1. Mathematics & Calculation Foundation:
   When working through basic arithmetic (percentages, ratios, time & work), do you
   calculate mostly in your head, or do you require rough scratchpad paper for simple
   two-digit multiplications and divisions?
   > Why this matters: Determines whether the morning math engine starts with
     arithmetic speed shortcuts (Vedic/mental math) or directly with conceptual drills.

2. General Science Recall (Class 9 & 10):
   If asked questions right now on basic Physics (Newton's laws, optics, circuits)
   and Chemistry (acids/bases, periodic table, reactions), is your recall completely
   blank, or do you remember the general concepts from school?
   > Why this matters: General Science represents ~30% of the CDS GK paper. If you
     retain basic concepts, we can rely entirely on rapid MCQ retrieval rather than
     re-reading textbooks.

3. Gujarati Administrative Reading Comfort:
   When you read this official sentence: 'અધિકારક્ષેત્ર ધરાવતા મેજિસ્ટ્રેટ સમક્ષ કાયદેસરની
   પ્રક્રિયા અનુસાર અટકાયત કરેલ વ્યક્તિને રજૂ કરવાની જોગવાઈ છે.' — did you understand it
   immediately, or did you have to pause to translate words like 'અધિકારક્ષેત્ર' or 'અટકાયત'?
   > Why this matters: Establishes whether the Lexicon Trainer needs foundational
     word-by-word drills or can jump directly into contextual sentence comprehension.

4. Actual Commute Internet Reliability:
   Does your daily metro route maintain stable 4G/5G mobile internet connectivity,
   or does the signal frequently drop underground?
   > Why this matters: Decides whether Metro Mode must be 100% bundled locally in
     memory/service workers or can pull dynamic data.

5. Running & Physical Baseline:
   What is your current running distance without stopping? Can you currently run
   5 kilometers continuously, and have you ever timed it?
   > Why this matters: The Gujarat PSI physical test requires a 5,000m run in
     25:00 minutes (pass/fail). If your baseline is zero, running must enter the
     weekly habit tracker immediately to prevent injury.

6. Primary Device for Metro Study:
   What exact smartphone (screen size / operating system) do you use during your metro commute?
   > Why this matters: Calibrates the thumb-reach zone, viewport heights, and
     touch target sizes for the one-handed Metro Mode.
